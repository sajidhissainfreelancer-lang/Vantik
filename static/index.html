Questions

What do I get back?

Your original Excel plus new columns: call status, interest level, requirement, callback time, next step, summary and a short remark.

Do the calls sound like a real person?

The AI speaks naturally, answers questions and handles objections. It always says it is an AI assistant if asked.

Can I call anyone?

No. You must have consent or a lawful right to contact every lead, and follow TRAI / DND rules. You accept this when you sign up.

Which language does it speak?

English for now. More languages can be added.

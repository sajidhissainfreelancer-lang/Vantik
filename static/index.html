Questions

How does the AI receptionist work?

You get an AI phone number. When you do not answer, your phone forwards the call to it, and the AI answers your customer using the details you gave us.

What do I see after each call?

A summary, the full conversation as text, the caller's number and a recording link, all in your dashboard.

Which phone companies work for call forwarding?

Call forwarding works with most Indian SIMs, including Airtel, Jio, Vi and BSNL. Your dashboard shows the steps for each.

Does the AI speak Tamil?

English is supported today. Tamil depends on the voice setup, so contact us to test it for your business.

What does it cost?

You pay a monthly fee for your AI number and then pay per minute of calls. Prices are shown in the pricing section of this page.
